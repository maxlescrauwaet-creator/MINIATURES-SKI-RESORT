Behind The Scenes

How We Make It

Every 1:32 scale snow cannon is crafted with precise detail. Here is a look at the workshop process from initial components to completed models.

STEP 01

Chassis & Trailer Rigging

Chassis frames, wheels, and stabilizer jacks are assembled in batches to ensure perfect 1:32 scale alignment.

STEP 02

Turbine & Valve Assembly

Propeller nozzles, compressor housings, decals, and safety labels are attached by hand.

STEP 03

Final Quality Check

Each individual cannon undergoes inspection on our assembly workbench before being securely boxed for delivery.
