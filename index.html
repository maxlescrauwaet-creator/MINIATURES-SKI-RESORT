Our Story

Miniatures Ski Resort started with a shared passion between Nikkes and WIIPY to bring the magic of alpine snowmaking and winter machinery down to 1:32 scale.

Every single model begins as a digital blueprint modeled directly after real-world TechnoAlpin, Demaclenko, and custom alpine snow generators. Using high-precision 3D printing and meticulous hand-finishing, we create collector-grade models tailored specifically for miniature ski resort dioramas.
