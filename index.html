Our Story

Miniatures Ski Resort started with a shared passion between Nikkes and WIIPY to bring the magic of alpine snowmaking machinery down to 1:32 scale.

Every model begins as a digital blueprint modeled directly after real-world TechnoAlpin and Demaclenko snow generators. Using high-precision 3D printing and meticulous hand-finishing, we create collector-grade models.
